## Brand & Style

This design system expresses compassionate clinical authority paired with tender educational warmth. Tailored for neurorehabilitation, pediatric therapies, and inclusive special education across Valledupar and Atánquez, the visual environment must alleviate parental anxiety while inspiring total clinical confidence.

The design movement combines **Contemporary Human-Centered Healthcare** with **Soft Tactile Realism**:
- Deep clinical integrity anchored by professional navy-cerulean tones.
- Sunlit therapeutic warmth conveyed through an inviting cream atmosphere and high-visibility energetic electric accents.
- Soft, generous structural geometry that avoids clinical coldness, evoking protective embrace and optimism.
- Dignified, accessible presentation celebrating cognitive and physical progress for families and multidisciplinary care teams.

## Colors

The palette operates on a strict functional ratio: **90% Warm Neutrals**, **7% Trust & Foundation Primary**, and **3% Vibrant Action Accent**.

- **Primary (`#1D5FA7`)**: Clinical trust blue. Reserved for top-level navigation, medical accreditation anchors, headline emphasis, primary clinical badges, and key institutional wayfinding.
- **Secondary / Action Accent (`#1D2BF2`)**: Vibrant electric indigo. Used intentionally for high-conversion entry points: immediate triage triggers, appointment booking, urgent contact chips, and micro-interaction focus states.
- **Tertiary (`#64D9CF`)**: Aqua teal tint. Used for soft ambient glows, supportive metric dividers, secondary service tags, and tranquil gradient transitions.
- **Neutral Background (`#FFFBF3`)**: Soft warm cream base. Prevents sterile hospital-grade eye fatigue while fostering a home-like, welcoming atmosphere.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure cloud white for isolated cards, modal sheets, and structured intake modules.
- **Text Primary (`#1F2A37`)**: Deep slate grey, delivering AAA readability over cream and white backgrounds without the stark harshness of pure black.
- **Text Muted (`#4B5563`)**: Balanced neutral grey for supporting clinical copy, accreditation disclaimers, and secondary metadata.

## Typography

The type system blends the organic warmth of **Outfit** for structural headings with the precision of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for medical service descriptions and interactive elements.

- **Headlines (Outfit)**: Friendly rounded terminal geometry that counters medical intimidation. Bold weights communicate safety and milestone achievements without aggressive sharpness.
- **Body & Captions (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Highly legible, open apertures engineered to remain clear for diverse parent age brackets, rural mobile screens, and low-contrast sunlight scenarios.
- Hierarchy is preserved through generous leading and weight differentiation rather than severe scale jumps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built upon a responsive 12-column fluid grid system bounded by a maximum desktop container width of `1240px`.

- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Desktop`: 1024px+ (12 columns, 24px gutters, 32px canvas margins)
  - `Tablet`: 768px – 1023px (8 columns, 20px gutters, 24px canvas margins)
  - `Mobile`: 320px – 767px (4 columns, 16px gutters, 20px canvas margins)

- **Vertical Rhythm**:
  - Sections maintain broad `space-3xl` breathing room on desktop (`space-2xl` on mobile) to allow reassuring psychological processing.
  - Component cards deploy `space-lg` to `space-xl` internal paddings, guaranteeing content separation without crowding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Ambient Tinted Shadows** combined with **Tonal Surface Framing**. The design strictly avoids clinical hard-edge drop shadows in favor of sunlight-diffused depth.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: `#FFFBF3` baseline background, accented with soft linear gradients fading into `rgba(100, 217, 207, 0.08)` across section boundaries.
- **Level 1 (Clinical Rest Cards)**: `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a dual shadow structure: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(31, 42, 55, 0.04)` and `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(31, 42, 55, 0.02)`. Border is a whisper-thin outline: `1px solid rgba(29, 95, 167, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Hover Cards)**: Elevated state upon hover: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(29, 95, 167, 0.08)`, gently translating the element `-4px` along the Y axis.
- **Level 3 (Action Sticky & Floating CTA)**: High-priority trigger: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(29, 43, 242, 0.32)`, imparting tactile prominence and immediate discoverability.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a reassuring curved physical language through Level 2 roundedness (`0.5rem` / `8px` baseline).

- **Standard Cards & Modals**: `1rem` (16px) corner radius, providing organic enclosure for therapy details and branch info.
- **Hero Containers & Visual Callouts**: `1.5rem` (24px) corner radius for safe framing of campus imagery and family testimonials.
- **Pill Badges & Buttons**: Full round `9999px` radius for interactive chips, phone triggers, branch selectors, and status indicators.
- Shapes express security, care, and approachability across all touchpoints.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Direct Intake)**:
  - Background `#1D2BF2`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`.
  - Fully rounded pill geometry (`9999px`), internal padding `0.875rem 1.75rem`.
  - Icon integration on leading edge (emergency triage phone or action mark).
  - Hover state: Background `#1622C4`, with elevation `Level 3`.
- **Secondary (Clinical Exploration & Portals)**:
  - Background `#1D5FA7`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`.
  - Rounded geometry (`0.75rem`), hover transitions to `#174C86`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**:
  - Transparent background, border `1.5px solid #1D5FA7`, text `#1D5FA7`.
  - Hover: background `rgba(29, 95, 167, 0.06)`.

### Badges & Chips
- **Service & Branch Filters (Valledupar / Atánquez)**:
  - Pill format (`9999px`), padding `0.375rem 1rem`.
  - Inactive: Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#4B5563`, border `1px solid rgba(31, 42, 55, 0.12)`.
  - Active: Background `#1D5FA7`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #1D5FA7`.
- **Clinical Accreditation Badges**:
  - Soft pastel pill: Background `rgba(100, 217, 207, 0.15)`, text `#1D5FA7`, font `label-sm`.

### Cards
- **Therapy & Service Cards**:
  - Background `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `1rem`, padding `1.75rem`.
  - Elevation `Level 1` moving smoothly to `Level 2` on hover.
  - Accentuated top indicator strip or badge for specialized pediatric care vs. adult physical rehab.
- **Branch Location Card (Dual Hub Architecture)**:
  - Dedicated cards distinguishing Valledupar (central modern clinical facility) and Atánquez (indigenous community-tailored outreach).
  - Integrates direct contact pills, localized maps, and operating schedules.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Triage & Contact Fields**:
  - Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid rgba(31, 42, 55, 0.15)`, radius `0.75rem`, padding `0.875rem 1.25rem`.
  - Typography `body-lg` in `#1F2A37`, placeholder `#9CA3AF`.
  - Focus state: border-color `#1D5FA7`, shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(29, 95, 167, 0.15)`, background `#FFFFFF`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `20px x 20px`, corner radius `6px` for checkboxes, fully circular for radio selections.
- Border `2px solid rgba(29, 95, 167, 0.3)`, checking state transitions to solid `#1D5FA7` with crisp white icon check.
